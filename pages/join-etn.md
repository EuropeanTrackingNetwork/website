### Why join ETN?

The European Tracking Network aims to deliver state of the art science based on the use of telemetry. We are a large network of scientists that share expertise, infrastructure, and knowledge. We collaborate widely; whether to establish the best ways to deploy receivers or tag animals, or to join forces for studies or meta-analyses. Subscribe to our mailing list to stay up to date on all our developments and exciting opportunities - we promise to keep the emails infrequent!
